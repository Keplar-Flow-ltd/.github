LightCheck

Clean power, chosen safely, kept running, on any phone.

A digital energy guidance platform delivered over USSD and SMS · keplarflow.org

The problem is not only lack of electricity

In underserved communities across Nigeria, a family saving toward solar cannot easily learn what size system they need, what it should cost, or who deserves their trust. LightCheck exists to change that. It is the information and decision infrastructure required to accelerate clean-energy adoption in underserved communities, delivered over the channel everyone already owns: any phone.

Four questions, four capabilities

1 · What do I need?

A short dial-in session in English or Hausa, a few questions on appliances, hours and spending. The SMS that comes back carries a recommended system size and its cost in cash, by instalment or pay-as-you-use. A guide, not a quote, and it says so.

2 · Who can I trust?

Providers that passed a defined verification standard, with prices, warranties and certification side by side. A provider only gets your number after an explicit yes.

3 · How do I keep it running?

Fault reports lead with a safety question, then rules-based triage, a matched checked technician, warranty-first routing and maintenance reminders.

4 · How do we plan better?

Anonymised answers build ward-level Community Energy Profiles, so developers, financiers and agencies can decide where and what to build on real demand data.

Works on any phone. No smartphone, no data plan, no app store. USSD and SMS reach the majority of rural adults who do not own an internet-enabled handset (GSMA 2025). Where there is no signal at all, a trained community energy agent runs the identical flow offline and syncs on coverage. LightCheck never charges users, and any network session charge is said plainly, before every session.

Roadmap

  • Now: a working menu-flow prototype. Next build deliverable is a live USSD sandbox; aggregator and short-code provisioning already under way.
  • Pilot, six months in Gwagwalada LGA, FCT: 500 completed assessments, 5 providers passing verification, 100 faults resolved, one ward-level Community Energy Profile shared. That proves Energy Guidance and seeds Provider Trust, Maintenance and Community Intelligence.
  • Direction: individual guidance, then community guidance, then market intelligence, then planning infrastructure. The destination is energy intelligence infrastructure, not a bigger app.
