Network

Become an early adopter

The network is a small circle of business owners and professionals who try what we build before anyone else sees it. Membership is by invitation, and we admit a few at a time.

Why join

Try it first. New tools reach the network before anyone else, including Liluts, our open-source little useful things.
Shape what we build. Your feedback changes the roadmap.
Trade notes. Owners and professionals in the network do similar work and compare scars.
Get recognized. The partners whose work adds the most value get credit for it, in writing.

Some of what we build ships in invite-only drops, FairDrops for one. Invitations go to the network first.

How it works

01

Write in: Send one email telling us what you do and where technology could help.

02

We talk: A short conversation to see where you fit.

03

Start building together: You get early tools, we get feedback, the work improves.

Ask for an invitation

There is no form on this page, on purpose. Membership runs on email and conversation, the way good work does. Write to hello@keplarflow.org with “Joining the network” in the subject line and a line about your work. We will take it from there.
