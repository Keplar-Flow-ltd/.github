# Keplar Flow Limited · Brand Kit
Assembled 2026-11-01. Everything needed for day-to-day brand ops: logos, colors,
fonts, web assets, the Decent Work Center YouTube pack, and the voice rules.

## What is inside
- 01-logos/ · the K mark (Option One): horizontal + vertical lockups and the K
  icon, each in SVG (vector, use these first) and @4x PNG, across the colorways
  (primary white, primary black, white, black, blue).
- 02-guidelines/ · Color Palette.png (the designer's palette board).
- 03-fonts/ · Montserrat.zip (body typeface, SIL Open Font License) and
  fixelPont-1-0.zip (display typeface, Bye Bye Binary CUTE license; the license
  PDF travels with the files, keep them together when sharing).
- 04-web/ · live-site assets: favicon set (192/512, apple-touch, .ico), OG
  share image, logo SVGs as deployed, brand photos (human-collab, the flow
  abstract), the dither corner motif, Montserrat + fixelPont webfonts (woff2),
  and site-tokens.css (the design-token source of truth).
- 05-decent-work-youtube/ · the Decent Work Center channel pack: banner,
  profile pictures, watermark, video templates, and the ready-to-paste channel
  metadata.
- 06-voice/ · the copy law: how Keplar Flow writes. Zero em dashes, no banned
  vocabulary, plain spoken English, claims carry receipts.

## The rules in one screen
- Colors: K-blue #194AFF is the accent. Surfaces run navy: #16213A to #0C1424
  (page backgrounds), deep navy #010814, surface #042052; ink #F2F6FF on dark,
  soft #CEDCF5, muted #9DB9EB. Full token list in 04-web/site-tokens.css.
- Type: Montserrat everywhere; fixelPont is display-only at 60px and above.
- Logo: prefer SVG. The K icon alone for tight surfaces; lockups for everything
  else. Clearspace = the K's stroke height; never stretch, recolor, or outline.
- Voice: plain, specific, human. Read the copy law before writing anything
  public. "Bring us the bottleneck" is the standing landing line.
- Fonts travel with their licenses: OFL for Montserrat, CUTE for fixelPont.
