## Logo files (which one when)
- Tight surfaces, avatars, watermarks: K icon (k-icon or "K icon - Blue").
- Documents, decks, email: horizontal lockup.
- Square layouts, merch: vertical lockup.
- Dark backgrounds: White or Primary White variants. Light: Black or Primary
  Black. Blue icon only on white or navy.

## Typography
- Montserrat: body and UI, all weights (variable font included).
- fixelPont: display only, 60px minimum, never for body text.
- Web: woff2 files in 04-web/.

## Voice (the copy law, shortest form)
- Zero em dashes. Periods, commas, colons, parentheses.
- No AI-tell vocabulary (seamless, unlock, leverage, empower, streamline,
  transformative, ecosystem, journey, and the rest of the banned list).
- One idea per sentence. Concrete beats abstract. Say it as you would across a
  table in Lagos. Headings are statements, not labels.
- Every public claim carries a receipt.
