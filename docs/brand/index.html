Voice · how Keplar Flow writes

  • Zero em dashes. Periods, commas, colons, parentheses.
  • No AI-tell vocabulary: seamless, unlock, leverage, empower, streamline, transformative, ecosystem, journey, and the rest of the banned list (full law in the zip).
  • One idea per sentence. Concrete beats abstract. Say it as you would across a table in Lagos.
  • Headings are statements, not labels.
  • Every public claim carries a receipt.
  • Standing landing line: "Bring us the bottleneck."
