About

Built for people, powered by systems

Keplar Flow is a software company based in Abuja, Nigeria.

Where we came from

Keplar Flow was founded in January 2026 by Emmanuel Demilade Adebayo, after years of running technology and engineering relationships in Nigeria. We started with one conviction: technology should make work better for the people doing it. Efficiency that grinds the team down is a bad trade. We do not make it.

Our mission

Ship software that puts people back inside their own operations, with real authority over the machines beside them.

What we believe

Ethical by default. We build systems so a person stays in charge.
Resilient by design. Built to keep working when the day does not go to plan.
Scaled with care. Growth that does not cost your team its dignity.
