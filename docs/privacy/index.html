Privacy notice

Short version: this website has no forms and no accounts. You email us, a person reads it, and we reply. This page explains what that means for your information.

What we collect

When you write to us, we receive your name, your email address, and whatever you put in the message. Nothing else arrives on our side.

What we do with it

We use your message to reply to you. We do not sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

How long we keep it

We keep the correspondence while the conversation is alive, then archive it.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask what we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@keplarflow.org and we will act on it.

Cookies and tracking

These pages set no cookies and run no advertising trackers.
