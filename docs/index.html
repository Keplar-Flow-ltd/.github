Decent Work Initiative

A practical commitment to fair work

Keplar Flow is driving a Decent Work agenda that helps founders, workers, and institutions align on dignity, productivity, equity, and long-term resilience in modern workplaces.

01 Clear principles for dignified and inclusive work design.
02 Responsible technology adoption across teams and communities.
03 Actionable standards for fair opportunities and outcomes.
04 Practical pathways for organizations ready to implement now.
Our position

Decent work is an engineering requirement

Our working paper on the future of decent work asks what fair, dignified, productive work means for founders, teams, and institutions — and how technology should serve it. We design every system we ship around that answer.
