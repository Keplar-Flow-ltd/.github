Human by design

The machines do the repeating. The people do the judging.

We build software for Industry 5.0. The last wave of automation pushed people to the edges of their own operations, and we think that was the wrong trade. Our systems put people back inside the work: in the loop, with real authority over the machines beside them. When a person’s contribution makes the system better, the value of that work comes back to them. That is what decent work means to us, and we build for it.

Ethical by default. Systems designed so the person stays in charge.
Resilient by design. Built to keep working when the day does not go to plan.
Scaled with care. Growth that does not cost your team its dignity.
What is Industry 5.0?

Industry 4.0 connected workplaces through automation and data. Industry 5.0 adds the human layer: technology that serves workers, supports sustainability, and stays resilient when conditions change.

Decent Work Initiative

What fair work looks like in practice

We run a Decent Work initiative at Keplar Flow. It asks one question: what does fair work look like now that software keeps doing more of it? We take the answers to founders and workers first, then to the institutions that set the rules. What comes out is practical: standards a company can start using this quarter.

01 Principles for dignified work, written in plain language.
02 How to roll out new technology without steamrolling the team.
03 Standards for fair chances and fair outcomes. Ones you can check.
04 A starting path for organizations ready to begin this quarter.
