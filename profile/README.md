# Keplar Flow Limited

We are a software company in Abuja, Nigeria. We build the platforms, automation,
and AI agents that take the busywork off your team.

[keplarflow.org](https://keplarflow.org) · hello@keplarflow.org

## The machines do the repeating. The people do the judging.

We build software for Industry 5.0. Automation carries the routine and a person
signs off on what counts. Teams work faster when they trust their tools, and
people stay when the work respects them.

## What we do

- **Platform engineering.** Cloud platforms and SaaS, built as an independent
  vendor. The platform grows with your business and stays yours.
- **Workflow automation.** We automate processes and integrate your ERP around
  the way your teams actually work.
- **Intelligent systems.** Agentic AI, IoT, and TinyML for decisions that cannot
  wait for a server. A person can still overrule the system.
- **Talent and learning.** We train the teams who run what we build. The
  knowledge stays on your payroll.

We also run a Decent Work initiative: plain-language standards for fair work,
written for founders and workers first.

## What we give away

Our small tools go out under the name **Liluts**, little useful things, as they
are ready for open source. Client and platform work stays private.
